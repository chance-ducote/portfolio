About Me

I am a software quality assurance professional with a diverse technical background spanning military service, software development, and project management. I served 13 years in the United States Navy as a Sonar Technician, working with advanced electronic and computer systems. My military experience helped me develop strong analytical, troubleshooting, leadership, and problem-solving skills.

I have three years of professional experience in manual software testing, quality assurance, and software project management. My work focuses on identifying software defects, verifying system functionality, and contributing to the delivery of reliable, high-quality software.

My educational background includes a Bachelor of Science in Web and Digital Design, with an emphasis on programming, web development, GUI design, and digital technologies. I enjoy expanding my technical knowledge and applying my skills to new challenges in software development, test automation, game development, and web design.
